Powering Solar Assets Beyond Installation.

Engineering discipline · structured O&M · performance management

Maitra Solar Solutions is a solar plant engineering company based in Dighi, Pune, delivering integrated solutions across project execution, operations and maintenance, asset management and technical assessment — for ground-mounted and rooftop solar plants across Maharashtra and Pan India.

From the first survey to handover, and from routine operation to complete overhaul, Maitra operates as a single accountable partner — combining site engineering discipline with structured O&M and data-backed performance management.
